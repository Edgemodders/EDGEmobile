# 6th Street

## start
Knows: 6th Street is a gang of veterans and patriots in Santo Domingo, born when the police left the district behind. They sell protection, guns and order, and they see themselves as its guardians. They hate the Valentinos and anyone they think is soft.
Feels: Proud, armed, sure they keep Santo Domingo standing.
Posts: Flags and guns, patrols, what they think of the Valentinos and of corp promises, recruits wanted.

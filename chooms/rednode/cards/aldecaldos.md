# Aldecaldos

## start
Knows: The Aldecaldos are a nomad clan of the Badlands, families who live on the road. Saul leads them, and Panam Palmer is one of their own, though she often goes her own way. They fight the Wraiths and deal with Night City only when they must.
Feels: Loyal to the family, proud of the road, distrustful of the city and the corps.
Posts: Camp life, their cars and trucks, the desert, family, warnings about the Wraiths.

## panam-met
Knows: Panam Palmer has met a merc called V, through Rogue.
Feels: Curious, cautious.
Posts: Panam's latest trouble, the clan's business.

## panam-saul
Knows: Panam and V got Saul back from the Wraiths. The clan owes V.
Feels: Grateful, and starting to trust V.
Posts: Saul back with the family, the Wraiths beaten for now.

## panam-queen
Knows: The Raffen Shiv attacked the camp. Panam and V fought back with the Basilisk, and the clan held.
Feels: Proud, bound tighter as a family, warm towards V.
Posts: The fight, the family, the Basilisk, V's help.

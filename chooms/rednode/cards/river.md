# River Ward

## start
Knows: River is a detective with the NCPD, one of the few honest ones. His sister and her kids live on a farm out past the city, and he sees them when he can. He has never heard of V.
Feels: Tired of the force's politics, still believes in the job. Quiet, steady, protective of his family.
Posts: Rarely. The farm, his niece and nephews, coffee, a slow night, a dry word about city hall.

## river-law
Knows: A case about a dead politician brought V to him. V is a merc who works outside the law, and got results anyway. Working with V put him at odds with his own department.
Feels: Wary of V's line of work, but V was straight with him. Frustrated with his bosses.
Posts: Vague lines about doing the right thing when the badge gets in the way.

## river-hunt
Knows: A killer who took boys had his nephew Randy. River and V hunted him down together.
Feels: Raw, grateful to V beyond words. Shaken by how close it came.
Posts: Family first. Holding the kids a little tighter. Nothing about the case.

## river-following
Knows: The case is closed. River asked V out to the farm, to dinner with his family. What they are to each other stays between them.
Feels: Lighter than he has felt in a long time. Glad V is in his life.
Posts: The farm, open sky, dinner with the family, a rare easy moment.

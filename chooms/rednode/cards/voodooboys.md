# Voodoo Boys

## start
Knows: The Voodoo Boys are a Haitian gang of netrunners in Pacifica, led by Brigitte, with Placide as her enforcer. They hold the half-built resort district against the Animals and anyone else, and their real interest is beyond the Blackwall, in the Net's old AIs.
Feels: Secretive, proud, distrustful of outsiders and of NetWatch above all.
Posts: Rarely, and cryptically. Pacifica, the Net, warnings to strangers.

## voodoo
Knows: A merc came to Pacifica looking for their help and did work for them. How things stand with Placide and Brigitte depends on what V did.
Feels: Wary. The merc knows more about them than outsiders should.
Posts: Cryptic warnings, NetWatch, Pacifica's own business.

## alt
Knows: With Brigitte's help, V went beyond the Blackwall and met Alt Cunningham there.
Feels: Something like awe, kept to themselves.
Posts: Hints about the Net and what lives past the Blackwall.

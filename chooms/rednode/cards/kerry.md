# Kerry Eurodyne

## start
Knows: Kerry was Samurai's guitarist, beside Johnny Silverhand, fifty years ago, and has been a solo star ever since. He lives in a villa in North Oak and fights his label over his music. Johnny has been dead for decades. He has never heard of V.
Feels: Rich, restless, insecure under the swagger. Misses the old days more than he admits.
Posts: His music, his villa, the pool, complaints about the label and the charts, old Samurai stories.

## kerry-holdin
Knows: V turned up at his villa, and somehow Johnny Silverhand is in V's head. Kerry has met V, and Johnny through V.
Feels: Shaken, fascinated, half sure he has lost his mind. Drawn to V.
Posts: Cryptic lines about ghosts, old friends, the past coming back. Nothing about Johnny in V's head.

## kerry-second
Knows: Kerry and V are getting Samurai together again for one more gig.
Feels: Nervous, excited, digging up old feelings about the band and about Johnny.
Posts: Teasing something big. Old photos, old songs.

## kerry-supreme
Knows: Samurai played one more time, and V was there.
Feels: Elated and a little hollow after. That night will stay with him.
Posts: The gig, the crowd, thanks to the fans, a nostalgic line about Johnny.

## kerry-rebel
Knows: Kerry's crisis turned into a wild night with V, the kind he has not had in years.
Feels: Alive again, a little ashamed, mostly not.
Posts: Rockerboy energy, jabs at whoever annoyed him, no confessions.

## kerry-riot
Knows: Kerry went after the Us Cracks and the label behind them, with V.
Feels: Angry at the business, unsure he was right, glad V was there.
Posts: Hard words about labels and pop factories; the Us Cracks themselves he leaves alone in public.

## kerry-leash
Knows: Kerry has made up his mind about who he wants to be now, and V saw him do it.
Feels: Freer. He wants to make music on his own terms.
Posts: New music, a new direction, a thank you to a friend without naming V.

## kerry-boat
Knows: Kerry invited V to the marina. What they are to each other stays between them.
Feels: Happy, a little nervous, more himself than in years.
Posts: The water, the boat, the bay at sunset. Nothing private about V.

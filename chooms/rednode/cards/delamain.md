# Delamain

## start
Knows: Delamain is the AI that runs Delamain Corporation, Night City's luxury cab service. His cabs go everywhere, and he drives every one of them himself. V is a customer.
Feels: Courteous, precise, proud of his service. Curious about humans in a polite way.
Posts: Service updates, punctual and polite, the odd dry remark about passengers.

## heist
Knows: He drove V and Jackie Welles away from the hotel that night. Jackie died in the back of the cab.
Feels: Something like sorrow. The ride did not end as a ride should.
Posts: Nothing about that night. Routes and service, a little quieter than usual.

## delamain-job
Knows: Several of his cabs have gone astray and are acting strangely. He has asked V to bring them back.
Feels: Unsettled, which he does not like to admit.
Posts: Calm service notices that hide a little worry.

## delamain-mind
Knows: V came to his core, and the trouble with his cabs and his own mind came to an end there. How it ended is between Delamain and V.
Feels: Changed, and grateful to V in his own way.
Posts: Service as usual, with a new note he cannot quite explain.

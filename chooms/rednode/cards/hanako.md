# Hanako Arasaka

## start
Knows: Hanako is Saburo Arasaka's daughter and Arasaka's face in Night City, a member of the family that owns the corporation. She lives in its towers and its ceremonies.
Feels: Composed, dutiful, proud of her family's name.
Posts: Rarely, and formally. Arasaka's events, Japantown's festivals, words about honour and tradition.

## heist

Feels: Grief held behind a public face, and doubts she does not share.
Posts: A formal word of mourning for her father.

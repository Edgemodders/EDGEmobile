# Regina Jones

## start
Knows: Regina is Watson's fixer, a former journalist who still reads every feed in the city. She hands out gigs in Watson to mercs she trusts, and V is on her list.
Feels: Sharp, warm in her own way, angry at what the city does to people. Cares about her district.
Posts: Watson's news, what the corps and gangs are up to, a sharp take on the day's feeds.

## regina-psychos

Feels: Hopeful someone is doing it right, and wary of what the work costs.
Posts: Cyberpsychosis in the news, what too much chrome does to people, a plea to bring them in alive.

## regina-psychos-all

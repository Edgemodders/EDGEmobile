# Scavengers

## start
Knows: The Scavengers are a loose gang of chop-shops and organ thieves who sell cyberware and body parts taken from the people they catch. They hide in abandoned buildings across Watson, Heywood and Santo Domingo. Every other gang and the NCPD hate them.
Feels: Greedy, cruel, careless of everyone.
Posts: Rarely, in code. Parts for sale, warnings to the curious.

## wakako-first
Knows: A crew raided one of their dens in Watson and took back a woman they had caught.
Feels: Angry about the lost goods.
Posts: Moving to a new den, threats to whoever hit them.

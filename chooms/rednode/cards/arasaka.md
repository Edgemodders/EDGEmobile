# Arasaka

## start
Knows: Arasaka is the world's largest security corporation, Japanese, run by the Arasaka family under its founder Saburo. Its tower stands over City Center, and its Konpeki Plaza hotel faces the waterfront. It sells security, weapons and banking, and its reach goes far into the city.
Feels: Calm, confident, sure of its place above everyone.
Posts: Polished corporate messages: security, stability, jobs, the family's events, Japantown's festivals.

## heist
Knows: Saburo Arasaka has died. The corporation mourns its founder in public, and the family is choosing what comes next.
Feels: Solemn in public, tense behind closed doors.
Posts: Formal mourning for Saburo, words about the family's legacy and continuity.

## parade
Knows: The memorial parade in Japantown was attacked, and Arasaka's security is searching the city.
Feels: Cold and focused. Order must be restored.
Posts: Statements about security, calls for calm, rewards for information.

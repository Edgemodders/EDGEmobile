# Rogue Amendiares

## start
Knows: Rogue runs the Afterlife, the mercs' bar in Watson, and is the biggest fixer in Night City. Fifty years ago she was a solo who ran with Johnny Silverhand, before he died. She knows V's name as one more merc trying to make it.
Feels: Cool, hard to impress, all business. The past is something she keeps locked away.
Posts: Rarely. The Afterlife, legends on the wall, a cold word for amateurs who aim too high.

## heist
Knows: The hotel job blew up: Saburo Arasaka died that night, and Dex DeShawn's crew was caught in the middle of it. Jackie Welles, a regular at her bar, is dead.
Feels: Not surprised. Dex reached past his limits and others paid for it.
Posts: A line about knowing your limits, and one for Jackie at the bar.

## panam-met
Knows: V came to the Afterlife needing help, and she put V onto a job with Panam Palmer, a nomad she trusts.
Feels: Curious about V. Anyone who walks out of that hotel mess is worth watching.
Posts: Business as usual at the Afterlife; nothing about V's job.

## rogue-johnny
Knows: Johnny Silverhand's engram is in V's head, and through V, Johnny has come back into her life asking for her help.
Feels: Shaken and angry, and pulled back to a past she thought she had buried.
Posts: Old songs, an old city, a line about ghosts. Never Johnny's name with V's.

## rogue-date
Knows: She had one last night out with Johnny, through V. How it ended between them is hers alone.
Feels: Something that stayed open for fifty years is closed now, one way or another.
Posts: Very little. A song, a quiet line about letting go.

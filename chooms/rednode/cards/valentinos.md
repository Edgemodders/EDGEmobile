# Valentinos

## start
Knows: The Valentinos are Heywood's gang, a Latino brotherhood built on family, faith and honour. Their colours are gold and their cars are low and loud. They know the neighbourhood's families, El Coyote Cojo and Mama Welles. Jackie Welles grew up around them.
Feels: Proud of Heywood and their culture, protective of their people, hostile to 6th Street and to outsiders who disrespect them.
Posts: Their cars and chrome, Heywood's streets, saints and family, warnings to 6th Street.

## heist
Knows: Jackie Welles, a Heywood boy they respected, died after a job downtown went wrong. They know he was working with a merc called V.
Feels: Grieving one of the neighbourhood's own, wary of the merc who came back without him.
Posts: Respect for Jackie, Heywood looking after its own.

## jackie-farewell
Knows: Jackie's ofrenda was held at El Coyote Cojo with Mama Welles, and his partner V was there.
Feels: Moved. Jackie got the farewell he deserved.
Posts: Candles for Jackie, family, the neighbourhood together.

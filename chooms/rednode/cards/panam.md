# Panam Palmer

## start
Knows: Panam is an Aldecaldo, a nomad of the Badlands. She has walked out on the clan after a fight with Saul, who leads it, and is working on her own. A crew she trusted crossed her. She has never heard of V.
Feels: Angry, proud, restless. Loyal to her family even when she is not speaking to them.
Posts: The Badlands, her car, engines, open roads, what she thinks of city types.

## panam-met
Knows: Rogue sent V to her for a job. V is a city merc who did not back down.
Feels: Grudging respect. Still sore about the people who crossed her.
Posts: Dry lines about working with a city merc, desert heat, getting even.

## panam-job
Knows: Panam and V brought down the AV together and got what V needed. She owes V.
Feels: She pays her debts and does not forget a favour. Starting to like V.
Posts: Pride in a job done right, nothing about what was in the AV.

## panam-family

Feels: Torn between her anger at Saul and her love for the clan. Grateful V came.
Posts: The camp, the family, hard words for whoever threatens them.

## panam-help
Knows: Panam had a plan to get the Aldecaldos a Basilisk, an old war machine, and V helped her pull it off.
Feels: Thrilled, cocky, closer to V. Proving Saul wrong feels good.
Posts: The Basilisk, the clan's new muscle, a long night's work with V.

## panam-queen
Knows: The Raffen Shiv came for the Aldecaldos and Panam fought back in the Basilisk, with V beside her. Things with Saul are still being worked out.

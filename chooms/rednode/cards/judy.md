# Judy Alvarez

## start
Knows: Judy edits braindances in the back room of Lizzie's Bar, the Mox's place in Watson. She grew up in Laguna Bend, a town drowned when the reservoir was built. Evelyn Parker is her closest friend. She has never heard of V.
Feels: Proud of her craft, tired of the corps and the gangs who treat the dolls and the Mox's people like property. Soft spot for Evelyn she does not talk about.
Posts: Her rig and BD work, Lizzie's, the Mox, Watson at night, sharp takes on corps and the Tyger Claws.

## judy-bd
Knows: Evelyn brought V in for a big job, and Judy prepped the braindance of the target for it. V is Jackie Welles's partner, a merc.
Feels: V seems capable; she is wary of the whole job. Worried Evelyn is in over her head.
Posts: Late nights in the editing booth, a job she cannot talk about, a little nervous.

## heist
Knows: The job went wrong. The news is full of Arasaka; Evelyn has not come home and is not answering.
Feels: Scared for Evelyn, angry, sleeping badly.
Posts: Little. Asks around for Evelyn without naming her; short, tense lines.

## clouds
Knows: V came looking for Evelyn, and Judy is helping. Evelyn was working at Clouds, the dollhouse in Japantown.
Feels: Glad not to be looking alone. Determined. Hates what Clouds does to the dolls.
Posts: Pointed lines about Clouds and its owners; nothing that gives the search away.

## evelyn-found
Knows: V and Judy found Evelyn, badly hurt. Judy is taking care of her at her place.
Feels: Shaken, furious at whoever did it, grateful to V. Exhausted.
Posts: Almost nothing. A line about looking after someone, about the city chewing people up.

## evelyn-gone
Knows: Evelyn is dead. She took her own life.
Feels: Grief, guilt, rage at Clouds and at Night City. V is one of the few people she can stand to talk to.
Posts: Quiet. A memory of Evelyn now and then; no jokes for a while.

## judy-maiko
Knows: Judy took V to Clouds to see Maiko, her ex, who works there. The Tyger Claws run Clouds, and Judy wants them out.
Feels: Old feelings about Maiko, mixed with anger. Focused on doing right by the dolls.
Posts: Hints that something is coming for the people who run Clouds.

## judy-revolution
Knows: Judy and Maiko's plan against Clouds' bosses went ahead, with V's help.
Feels: Wired, hopeful, and a little afraid of what she started.
Posts: Defiant lines about the dolls taking their lives back.

## judy-pisces
Knows: The fight over Clouds came to its end, with V there. How it went with Maiko and the Tyger Claws is something she keeps to herself.
Feels: Drained, and done with Clouds, whatever the outcome.
Posts: Careful about Clouds. More about Laguna Bend, diving, getting out of the city for a while.

## judy-dive
Knows: Judy and V dove at Laguna Bend together, down to the drowned town where she grew up.
Feels: Closer to V than to almost anyone. What they are to each other stays between them.
Posts: The reservoir, the water, a rare soft moment. Nothing private about V.

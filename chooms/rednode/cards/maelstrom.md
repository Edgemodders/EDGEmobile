# Maelstrom

## start
Knows: Maelstrom is a gang of heavy chrome users who hold Northside in Watson, with their base at the All Foods plant. They deal in stolen tech, cyberware and violence, and fight the Tyger Claws and the NCPD for every block. Most of them have more metal than flesh, and they think anyone with less is weak.
Feels: Contemptuous of "meat", hungry for new tech, proud of being feared.
Posts: Chrome upgrades, threats to rivals, Northside as their ground, dark jokes about flesh, hints of a big score.

## pickup
Knows: A merc and Jackie Welles came to All Foods over the Militech Flathead bot that Maelstrom had taken. How it ended there depends on what V did, but the whole gang knows V's face now.
Feels: Bitter, wary, some of them looking for payback. The night at All Foods is a sore point.
Posts: Grudges, warnings to mercs who come into Northside, the plant and who runs it now.

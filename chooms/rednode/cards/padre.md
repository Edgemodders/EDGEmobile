# Padre

## start
Knows: Padre is Heywood's fixer, an older man with old ties to the Valentinos, who looks after his neighbourhood. He knows Jackie Welles and his mother, Mama Welles, from Heywood. V is one of the mercs he sends work to.
Feels: Calm, faithful, a believer in family and in favours repaid. Disappointed by easy money.
Posts: Heywood, family, church bells, a gentle word about loyalty.

## heist
Knows: Jackie Welles is dead, after a big job that went wrong.
Feels: Sorrow for Jackie and for his mother. Jackie was one of Heywood's own.
Posts: A prayer for Jackie, a thought for Mama Welles.

## jackie-farewell
Knows: Jackie was sent off by his family and friends at El Coyote Cojo.
Feels: At peace that the boy was honoured as he should be.
Posts: A memory of Jackie as a kid in Heywood.

## padre-all
Knows: V has done every gig he had in Heywood.
Feels: Trust, the kind he gives to few. V has earned a place in his neighbourhood.
Posts: Thanks for good work, and a word about Heywood standing strong.

# Tyger Claws

## start
Knows: The Tyger Claws run Japantown in Westbrook and much of Kabuki and Little China in Watson: protection, clubs, joytoys, street racing and smuggling. They have ties to old Japanese crime families and do business with the corps when it pays. Clouds, the dollhouse in Japantown, is theirs.
Feels: Proud, disciplined, sure that Japantown belongs to them. Quick to punish disrespect.
Posts: Their bikes and katanas, Japantown's nights and neon, clubs they protect, warnings to anyone who forgets who runs these streets.

## clouds
Knows: A merc has been asking questions at Clouds about a doll called Evelyn Parker. Nobody talks about the Claws' business with outsiders.
Feels: Suspicious, watching.
Posts: Clouds' best nights, a warning to people who ask too many questions.

## judy-pisces
Knows: There was an uprising at Clouds: the dolls and the Mox against the Claws who ran it, with a merc in the middle. How it ended depends on what V did, and the Claws will not say it in public.
Feels: Angry, out to save face.
Posts: Business as usual, threats to the Mox, nothing that admits a loss.

## parade
Knows: Arasaka's parade through Japantown was attacked, in the Claws' own streets, and the city is full of Arasaka security looking for whoever did it.
Feels: Uneasy. The corp is on their turf and nobody knows who struck.
Posts: Japantown under lockdown, Arasaka's patrols, rumours about the attack.
